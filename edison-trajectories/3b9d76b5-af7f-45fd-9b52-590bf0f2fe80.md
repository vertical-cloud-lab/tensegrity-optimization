## Major Venues for Presenting Novel Work on Tensegrity Structures

Tensegrity structures—prestressed frameworks of discontinuous compression elements (struts) connected by continuous tension elements (cables)—are a multidisciplinary research area spanning structural engineering, robotics, space systems, biomechanics, and architecture. Below is a comprehensive guide to the major conferences, symposia, and other venues where novel tensegrity work can be presented, along with key researchers to contact.

---

### 1. ROBOTICS-FOCUSED CONFERENCES

**1.1 IEEE International Conference on Robotics and Automation (ICRA)**
- **Typical dates:** May (annually)
- **Recent edition:** ICRA 2025 was held in May 2025 (kazoleas2024designandfabrication pages 7-7, guachetaalba2023newapproachesand pages 10-11)
- **Abstract deadline:** Typically September of the preceding year
- **Relevance:** ICRA is a premier venue for tensegrity robotics. Multiple tensegrity papers have appeared at ICRA, including work on SUPERball tensegrity locomotion, tensegrity spines with CPG-based control, tensegrity robot duct exploration (DuCTT), and bionic ankle tensegrity exoskeletons (kazoleas2024designandfabrication pages 7-7). Recent 2025 papers included Wei et al.'s BATE bionic ankle tensegrity exoskeleton and Kazoleas et al.'s TensiWorm peristaltic tensegrity robot.
- **Key contacts:** Kostas Bekris (Rutgers University), Rebecca Kramer-Bottiglio (Yale University), Alice M. Agogino (UC Berkeley), Adrian K. Agogino (NASA Ames) (wang2022arecurrentdifferentiable pages 1-2, kim2020rollinglocomotionof pages 1-2)

**1.2 IEEE/RSJ International Conference on Intelligent Robots and Systems (IROS)**
- **Typical dates:** October (annually)
- **Recent edition:** IROS 2025, October 2025
- **Abstract deadline:** Typically March of the same year
- **Relevance:** IROS regularly features tensegrity robot papers on manipulation, locomotion, and soft robotics. Recent 2025 papers included Herrmann et al.'s compliant tensegrity robotic arm and Yoshimitsu et al.'s uncertainty-aware motion planning for tensegrity manipulators.
- **Key contacts:** Kostas Bekris (Rutgers), Shuhei Ikemoto, Valter Böhm (OTH Regensburg) (wang2022arecurrentdifferentiable pages 1-2)

**1.3 IEEE International Conference on Soft Robotics (RoboSoft)**
- **Typical dates:** April (annually)
- **Recent edition:** RoboSoft 2025, April 2025 (mortensen2025tensegritybasedrobotleg pages 1-2)
- **Abstract deadline:** Typically October–November of the preceding year
- **Relevance:** Tensegrity robots are a core topic within soft robotics. Mortensen et al. presented their tensegrity-based robot leg with variable stiffness at RoboSoft 2025, with all authors from EPFL's Laboratory of Intelligent Systems (mortensen2025tensegritybasedrobotleg pages 1-2).
- **Key contacts:** Dario Floreano (EPFL), Erik Mortensen (EPFL)

### 2. MECHANICAL ENGINEERING CONFERENCES

**2.1 ASME International Design Engineering Technical Conferences (IDETC) – Mechanisms and Robotics Conference (MR)**
- **Typical dates:** August (annually)
- **Recent edition:** 48th Mechanisms and Robotics Conference, August 2024 (kazoleas2024designandfabrication pages 7-7)
- **Abstract deadline:** Typically February–March of the same year
- **Relevant sessions:** The Mechanisms and Robotics Conference (MR) within IDETC is a longstanding venue for tensegrity work. Correa, Duffy, and Crane presented static analysis of tensegrity structures at the ASME Mechanisms Conference as early as 2002 (gomezjauregui2023tensegrityapplicationsto pages 37-40, gomezjauregui2023tensegrityapplicationsto pages 34-37). Kazoleas et al. presented their deployable tensegrity millirobot at the 48th MR in 2024 (kazoleas2024designandfabrication pages 7-7).
- **Key contacts:** Sichen Yuan, Muhao Chen (University of Kentucky, Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering) (zhao2025modularshapechangingtensegrityblocks pages 1-2)

**2.2 ASME Conference on Smart Materials, Adaptive Structures and Intelligent Systems (SMASIS)**
- **Typical dates:** September (annually)
- **Abstract deadline:** Typically March–April
- **Relevance:** Covers active/adaptive tensegrity structures, smart material integration, and shape control. Tensegrity work with shape memory alloys and deployable structures has appeared here.

### 3. STRUCTURAL ENGINEERING AND SPATIAL STRUCTURES

**3.1 IASS Annual Symposium (International Association for Shell and Spatial Structures)**
- **Typical dates:** Late August/September (annually)
- **Recent editions:** IASS 2024 "Redefining the Art of Structural Design," August 26–30, 2024, Zurich, Switzerland, edited by Philippe Block, Giulia Boller, Catherine DeWolf, Jacqueline Pauli, and Walter Kaufmann (pontello2024designtoproductionprocessfor pages 1-3). IASS 2023 featured the "Integration of Design and Fabrication" theme (loh2023suspendedtensegritythe pages 10-12).
- **Abstract deadline:** Typically January–March of the same year
- **Relevant sessions:** Tensegrity structures, form-finding, deployable structures, cable-membrane systems, structural art. Recent tensegrity presentations include Pontello & Maffei on large-scale inflated tensegrity artwork and Loh et al. on suspended anthropomorphic tensegrity machines (loh2023suspendedtensegritythe pages 10-12, pontello2024designtoproductionprocessfor pages 1-3).
- **Key contacts:** Andrea Micheletti (tensegrity form-finding), Valentín Gómez-Jáuregui (University of Cantabria, tensegrity applications review author) (gomezjauregui2023tensegrityapplicationsto pages 37-40, obara2025assessmentoftensegrity pages 93-95)

### 4. SPACE AND AEROSPACE CONFERENCES

**4.1 AIAA SciTech Forum**
- **Typical dates:** January (annually)
- **Abstract deadline:** Typically June of the preceding year
- **Relevance:** Tensegrity deployable structures for space applications, morphing airfoils. Zhao et al. presented a lightweight robotic tensegrity morphing airfoil at AIAA SciTech 2026. Tibert presented deployable tensegrity configurations at AIAA Structures, Structural Dynamics and Materials conferences (obara2025assessmentoftensegrity pages 41-43).
- **Key contacts:** Muhao Chen (University of Kentucky), Luyang Zhao (Dartmouth College) (zhao2025modularshapechangingtensegrityblocks pages 1-2)

**4.2 IEEE Aerospace Conference**
- **Typical dates:** March (annually), Big Sky, Montana
- **Relevance:** NASA tensegrity rover concepts and deployable lunar tower designs have been presented here. Miller et al. presented self-deploying lunar tower designs at IEEE Aerospace 2023.
- **Key contacts:** NASA Langley Research Center deployable structures group

### 5. SMART STRUCTURES AND ACTIVE CONTROL

**5.1 SPIE Smart Structures + NDE (Nondestructive Evaluation)**
- **Typical dates:** March (annually), typically Long Beach or San Diego, California
- **Abstract deadline:** Typically August–October of the preceding year
- **Relevant sessions:** "Electroactive Polymer Actuators and Devices (EAPAD)" and "Active and Passive Smart Structures and Integrated Systems." Chan, Arbelaez, Bossens, and Skelton presented "Active vibration control of a three-stage tensegrity structure" at SPIE's 11th Annual International Symposium on Smart Structures and Materials in March 2004 (gomezjauregui2023tensegrityapplicationsto pages 34-37). Osikowicz et al. presented experimental shape control of cylindrical tensegrity structures at SPIE 2022, and Agoramurthy et al. presented deployable tensegrity structures for seismic hazard mitigation (obara2025assessmentoftensegrity pages 205-207).
- **Key contacts:** Robert E. Skelton (UC San Diego, pioneer in tensegrity mechanics and control)

### 6. BIO-INSPIRED AND EXOSKELETON APPLICATION CONFERENCES

**6.1 IEEE International Conference on Cyborg and Bionic Systems (CBS)**
- **Typical dates:** October (annually)
- **Relevance:** Lehmann et al. presented biomimetic auxetic compliant tensegrity metamaterial for soft robotic skin at CBS 2025.
- **Key contacts:** Valter Böhm, Lukas Lehmann, David Herrmann, Leon Schaeffer (all OTH Regensburg, Faculty of Mechanical Engineering) (schaeffer2023theoreticalconsiderationson pages 1-3)

**6.2 IEEE International Conference on Rehabilitation Robotics (ICORR) / WeRob**
- **Typical dates:** Biennial or annual, varies
- **Relevance:** Tensegrity-based exoskeletons for rehabilitation are an emerging topic. Ni et al. designed an upper limb bionic exoskeleton rehabilitation device based on tensegrity structure (ni2024designandcontrol pages 1-2). Wei et al. have published on the BATE bionic ankle tensegrity exoskeleton in IEEE TMRB and IEEE/ASME TMECH.
- **Key contacts:** Dunwen Wei, Fanny Ficuciello; Peng Ni and Jianwei Sun (Changchun University of Technology) (ni2024designandcontrol pages 1-2)

**6.3 CAADRIA (Association for Computer-Aided Architectural Design Research in Asia)**
- **Typical dates:** March–April (annually)
- **Relevance:** Hsieh and Hou presented a biomimetic robotic system with tensegrity-based compliant mechanism at CAADRIA 2024.
