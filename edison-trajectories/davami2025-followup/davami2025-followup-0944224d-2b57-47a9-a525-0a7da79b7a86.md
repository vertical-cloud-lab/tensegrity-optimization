Visual evidence for the most Davami‑adjacent dynamic/impact paper in the retrieved set (Pajunen et al., 2019) includes the drop‑weight impact setup and force–time response (Figure 2) and the residual strain after repeated impacts (Table 1), supporting that work’s dynamic testing methodology and reusability claims (pajunen2019designandimpact media 7fde5c0a, pajunen2019designandimpact media 1f2c5bd6).

Positioning for a BYU MRG / IDETC project (PLA struts + TPU tendons, multi‑material FDM, multifidelity Bayesian optimization): the most direct gap relative to the above literature is the combination of (1) true tendon–strut material contrast implemented by dual‑material printing (rather than monolithic “printed cables” or resin compliant hinges), and (2) explicit data‑efficient optimization of tensegrity geometry/material assignment/prestress for impact energy absorption (rather than one‑off parametric iteration or single‑objective numerical sizing). The Amendola et al. bi‑material EBM+Spectra work demonstrates the dynamic importance of real flexible cables but requires manual post‑tensioning (amendola2015ontheadditive pages 1-3, amendola2018onthemechanical pages 1-2), while Yavas et al. validates PLA/TPU multi‑material FFF as an energy‑absorption enabler but not in a true tensegrity architecture with discrete tendons (yavas2022designandfabrication pages 1-2, yavas2022designandfabrication pages 2-3). Bauer et al. and Zhang et al. show that member‑type assignment and cross‑section/prestress optimization can strongly amplify energy absorption, but they do not address FDM PLA/TPU tendonized lattices under impact (bauer2021tensegritymetamaterialstoward pages 6-7, bauer2021tensegritymetamaterialstoward pages 8-9, zhang2021optimizationforenergy pages 1-2). Pajunen et al. (2019/2021) provide the closest AM + dynamics/waves baseline for monolithic tensegrity‑inspired architectures, against which BYU can cleanly quantify the effect of introducing TPU tendons (pajunen2019designandimpact pages 5-7, pajunen2021prestraininducedbandgaptuning pages 2-3).
