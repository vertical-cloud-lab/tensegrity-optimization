\section{Joint-Design Study}
\label{si:joints}
% -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
The strut-to-cable junction was developed and ranked through a five-design
OpenSCAD study (anchor-bulb, dovetail, TPU-sleeve overmold, eyelet-loop, and
TPU-rebar variants). The working prototype uses a dovetail joint (Design~B)
with an anchor-bulb backup (Design~A) for sensitivity studies; a captive TPU
core is routed inside the PLA shell so that the soft tendon is protected from
layer-line failure at the transition.

Full results, renders, and the Phase-3/Phase-4 design reviews are documented in
\href{\repo/pull/39}{PR~\#39} (joint design) and
\href{\repo/pull/35}{PR~\#35} (CAD parameterization and print history).

\section{Support-Generation Workflow for Soft Members}
\label{si:supports}
% -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
Near-vertical TPU tendons are otherwise unsupported during printing. The slicer
profile combines a tensegrity-specific Bambu Studio recipe (support threshold
angle lowered from \SI{40}{\degree} to \SI{10}{\degree}, support material
matched to the rigid extruder) with manually generated narrowing pillars that
ray-cast against the printable mesh underside, producing TPU-safe coverage of
vertical members without slicer-side painting.

The manual narrowing-pillar workflow and the threshold-angle recipe are
documented in \href{\repo/pull/66}{PR~\#66}; the H2D multi-part material
assignment fix is in \href{\repo/pull/64}{PR~\#64}; the single-batch Sobol
plate-layout generator is in \href{\repo/pull/65}{PR~\#65}.

\section{Drop-Tower Characterization Notes}
\label{si:drop}
% -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
The primary drop fixture is a bungee-assisted laboratory drop tower whose base
accelerates downward faster than \(1g\); unconstrained specimens lift off the
base during descent by design of the rig, so the protocol constrains the
specimen top through light tethers and registers the base. Protocol details,
the accelerometer-tuning campaign, and the impact-window analysis are recorded
in \href{\repo/pull/67}{PR~\#67},
\href{\repo/issues/71}{issue~\#71}, and
\href{\repo/pull/74}{PR~\#74}.
