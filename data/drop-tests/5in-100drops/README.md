# 5-in 100-drop campaign on `RW5F61`

Raw TP4 accelerometer exports for the **5-in-height 100-drop campaign** by
@ctrhjk, posted on
[PR #67](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/tensegrity-optimization/pull/67)
(TP4 session `5 in height 100 drops`).

The one change under test vs the CH4-trigger 50-drop campaign: the **drop
height was lowered from 13 in to 5 in** — the "Option A" fix for the
under-ranged low-range bottom tri-axis (CH6–8, ~1 kG full scale), which
exceeded its full scale on 85/100 (13-in 100-drop run) and 40/50
(CH4-trigger run) drops. Everything else is unchanged: specimen **`RW5F61`**
(cumulative history now 280 conducted / 277 captured drops), trigger on CH4
at 1000 G, taped CH5 on the base plate, and the **housing entrances taped**
on both key-seats to retain the tri-axis sensors.

## Recording setup

Drop height **5 in**, drops released automatically (median 12 s cadence,
range 11–12 s; the 100 events span ~19 min on 07/08/2026). DAQ as in the
prior series: 200 ms record, 125 kHz (8 µs), 25000 samples, 2 % (4 ms)
pre-trigger. All channels AC-coupled, ICP on, Half-Sine analysis.

## Files

`raw/5in_100drops_Signal{1..100}.csv` — TP4 Time-Domain exports (header
block, then `Time, CH2..CH8 (G's)`), renamed from the as-posted
`5 in drops_Signal*.csv` (spaces → underscores). The 100 CSVs were posted as
four zip archives of 25 files each (`drop{1..4}.zip`) to fit GitHub's
25 MB-per-attachment limit — see the data-handling section of
[`docs/drop-test-protocol.md`](../../docs/drop-test-protocol.md).

**All 100 captures are real drops** — no spurious triggers, no sensor
fall-offs; the CH4 trigger crossing sits at 3.896 ± 0.002 ms in every
record (±1 sample of jitter).

Known data issue: the drop-axis bottom channel **CH8 no longer exceeds its
989.1 G full scale (0/100 over, max 93.2 % FS)** — the goal of the height
change — but its raw peak is suspiciously constant (885–922 G, CV 0.4 %,
while every other channel varies 13–25 % drop-to-drop), i.e. the low-range
unit is likely still amplitude-limiting just below full scale. Treat
BOT-derived amplitudes as qualitative pending a datasheet linear-range check.
