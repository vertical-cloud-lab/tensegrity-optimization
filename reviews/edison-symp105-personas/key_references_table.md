|---|---|---|
| Shinde et al., 2022, *Towards an Ideal Energy Absorber* | Energy Absorption Metrics/Baselines | Defines and compares the key crashworthiness metrics the abstract currently omits—SEA, maximum transmitted stress, plateau behavior, and densification efficiency—across honeycomb, lattice, and TPMS baselines; essential for Reviewer A’s demand for quantitative benchmarking (pqac-00000000, pqac-00000001, pqac-00000002) |
| Tuninetti et al., 2025, *Biomimetic Lattice Structures* | Energy Absorption Metrics/Baselines | Summarizes how hierarchical and bio-inspired lattices improve SEA, crushing force efficiency, and plateau stress relative to conventional architectures; useful for arguing why a new unit cell is mechanistically justified (pqac-00000003) |
| Bustihan & Botiz, 2026, *Recent Advances in AM Polymeric Structures* | Energy Absorption Metrics/Baselines | Provides broad SEA ranges, topology-performance trends, and cautions about nonstandard testing; valuable for calibrating claims about polymeric lattices and for comparing against foams and lattices in the literature (pqac-00000004, pqac-00000005, pqac-00000006) |
| Bates et al., 2016, *3D Printed Polyurethane Honeycombs* | FDM Polymer Energy Absorption | A foundational FFF/TPU energy-absorption paper showing orientation and strain-rate sensitivity, elastic recovery, and comparison to polyurethane foams; directly relevant to the abstract’s TPU/FDM prototype claims (pqac-00000036, pqac-00000037) |
| Bustihan et al., 2025, *Reusable 3D-Printed TPU Honeycombs* | FDM Polymer Energy Absorption | Demonstrates how TPU grade and geometry influence energy absorption efficiency and crushing load efficiency in FDM honeycombs; a strong baseline the authors should compare against if they claim orientation-robust impact mitigation (pqac-00000032, pqac-00000034) |
| León-Calero et al., 2021, *3D Printing of Thermoplastic Elastomers* | FDM Polymer Energy Absorption | Shows that TPU/TPE energy absorption depends strongly on infill pattern and density, reinforcing that process and internal architecture must be reported rigorously for any TPU/PLA claims (pqac-00000035) |
| Ye et al., 2019, *PLA Lattice Core Sandwich Structures* | FDM Polymer Energy Absorption | Offers a PLA-specific energy-absorption baseline and demonstrates that reported SEA values depend strongly on architecture and loading mode; useful because the abstract invokes PLA without benchmarking it (pqac-00000035) |
| Pajunen et al., 2019, *Design and Impact Response of 3D-Printable Tensegrity-Inspired Structures* | Tensegrity Impact Absorption | One of the most directly relevant papers: experimentally characterizes impact response, load-limiting behavior, and reusability of tensegrity-inspired printed structures; should be a central citation for any tensegrity-impact abstract (pqac-00000011, pqac-00000028) |
| Zhang et al., 2021, *Optimization for Energy Absorption of 3D Tensegrity Lattice* | Tensegrity Impact Absorption | Directly addresses optimization of a 3D tensegrity lattice for energy absorption, including the rationale for tensegrity as a lightweight aerospace energy absorber; highly relevant to the abstract’s optimization framing (pqac-00000008) |
| Goyal et al., 2019, *Analytical Study of Tensegrity Lattices* | Tensegrity Impact Absorption | Provides analytical support for mass-efficient energy absorption in tensegrity systems for lander-type applications; useful for judging whether tensegrity is actually mass-competitive versus simpler absorbers (pqac-00000009) |
| Garanger et al., 2021, *Soft Tensegrity Systems for Planetary Landing* | Tensegrity Impact Absorption | Connects tensegrity directly to planetary landing and shows why buckling-tolerant designs matter under highly dynamic events; important for lander framing, but also a reminder that realistic dynamic mechanics must be addressed (pqac-00000010) |
| Vangelatos et al., 2021, *Strength through Defects* | Bayesian Optimization of Lattices | A landmark architected-materials BO paper showing how objectives, mixed variables, uncertainty, and sample efficiency should be formulated; a useful model for Reviewer A’s critique of the abstract’s under-specified BO loop (pqac-00000016, pqac-00000018, pqac-00000023) |
| Mo et al., 2023, *Accelerated Design of Architected Materials with Multifidelity BO* | Bayesian Optimization of Lattices | Particularly important because it optimizes energy absorption with constraints on plateau stress variation and relative density using experiments plus simulations; exactly the kind of rigorous BO framing missing from the abstract (pqac-00000017, pqac-00000019, pqac-00000020, pqac-00000021, pqac-00000022) |
| Cronau & Engstler, 2025, *Energy Absorption of 3D Printed Stochastic Lattices under Impact Loading* | Dynamic Testing / Rate Effects | Shows why quasi-static results cannot simply stand in for impact conditions and details dynamic drop-tower testing at relevant strain rates; essential for evaluating the validity of the abstract’s drop-testing claims (pqac-00000025, pqac-00000027) |
| Fasanella, 2009, *Multiterrain Earth Landing Systems* | Mars EDL Heritage | A concise survey of heritage landing attenuation technologies, including Pathfinder/MER airbags and crushable systems; indispensable for Reviewer B’s requirement that new concepts be compared against what actually flew (pqac-00000012, pqac-00000015) |
| Cadogan et al., 2002, *Mars Pathfinder Inflatable Airbag Landing System* | Mars EDL Heritage | Core Pathfinder heritage reference documenting the development and evaluation of the airbag landing system; should be cited if the authors invoke Mars payload protection at all (pqac-00000012) |
| Mennu et al., 2024, *Passive Energy Absorbing Composite Structure for Planetary Payloads* | Mars EDL Heritage | A recent planetary-landing passive absorber study using honeycomb/crush mechanisms and peak-acceleration metrics; a strong contemporary comparator for any lander-oriented passive impact absorber (pqac-00000013, pqac-00000014) |
| Aliberti et al., 2026, *AM of Polymers and Composites for Aerospace* | Flight Materials | Important for Reviewer B because it clarifies that aerospace AM polymers for harsh environments are high-performance systems such as PEEK/PEI/Ultem—not PLA/TPU—and that environmental durability is a real gating issue (pqac-00000024) |
| Heltzel et al., 2008, *Degradation of Thermal Control Materials* | Flight Materials | Useful background on vacuum/thermal-cycling degradation and outgassing concerns in space materials; supports the critique that flight-environment durability cannot be hand-waved in Mars lander framing (pqac-00000024) |
| Wu et al., 2026, *Energy Absorption and Rebound of 3D-Printed TPU Lattices* | Orientation Dependence / Landing Robustness | Highlights orientation-dependent and anisotropic behavior in TPU lattice systems aimed at landing/impact use cases, making it highly relevant to the abstract’s claim of “consistent impact absorption regardless of landing orientation” (pqac-00000030) |
