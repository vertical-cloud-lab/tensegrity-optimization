# Round-3 print recipe

Generated by `python bo/t3_prism_bo_campaign.py --round 3 --batch-number 3`. Every article is specified at 20.23 g as printed; the scale column is what gets that mass at that article's own strut infill, so a sparse article is a larger one.

## Filament settings, one value for the whole batch

- PLA at 211 C and 15.8 mm^3/s (85 mm/s at the profile's 0.62 x 0.30 mm bead)
- TPU at 247 C and 4.8 mm^3/s (26 mm/s)

These four are filament settings, so a print job carries exactly one value of each. Set them once and print every plate of this batch with them: formally they are the print parameters of trial 31, the batch's best-predicted specimen, applied everywhere. With all 17 tested articles at a single point of the process space there is no data to prefer one setting over another, so the values were drawn by Sobol sampling within the bounds (see the README) rather than asked of the model.

**Before slicing, switch the TPU filament preset.** Both tested plates carry `Bambu TPU 85A @BBL H2D 0.4 nozzle`, left over from before the high-flow hotend went in, and it caps the temperature window at 240 C, below what this batch asks for. Switch to `Bambu TPU 85A @BBL H2D`, whose window is 200 to 250 C. Typing the number into the old preset is not the same thing.

The two infill densities are per part, set by right-clicking the part in the object list and adding a sparse infill density override: the `-struts` part runs on extruder 1 (PLA), the `-cables` part on extruder 2 (TPU). Without those overrides both parts inherit the one global value and the batch loses its two per-article process axes.

Articles whose end-cap radius comes out below 20.1 mm are flagged `end cap`. That is the smallest article printed so far, and the three igloo mounts and three key-seats are absolute-size, so check in the slicer that they still fit before printing.
