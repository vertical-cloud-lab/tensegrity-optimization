# Round-5 print recipe

Generated by `python bo/t3_prism_bo_campaign.py --round 6 --batch-number 5`. Every article is specified at 20.23 g as printed; the scale column is what gets that mass at that article's own strut infill, so a sparse article is a larger one.

## Filament settings, one value for the whole batch

- PLA at 223 C and 24.7 mm^3/s (133 mm/s at the profile's 0.62 x 0.30 mm bead)
- TPU at 240 C and 3.0 mm^3/s (16 mm/s)

These four are filament settings, so a print job carries exactly one value of each. Set them once and print every plate of this batch with them: formally they are the print parameters of trial 55, the batch's best-predicted specimen, applied everywhere. Every batch prints at one filament point, so the batch-level points measured so far are confounded with everything else that changed between rounds; a model-preferred setting is therefore not identified, and the values continue the seeded Sobol sequence over the bounds (see the README) rather than being asked of the model.

The two infill densities are per part, set by right-clicking the part in the object list and adding a sparse infill density override: the `-struts` part runs on extruder 1 (PLA), the `-cables` part on extruder 2 (TPU). Without those overrides both parts inherit the one global value and the batch loses its two per-article process axes.

Articles whose end-cap radius comes out below 20.1 mm are flagged `end cap`. That is the smallest article printed so far, and the three igloo mounts and three key-seats are absolute-size, so check in the slicer that they still fit before printing.
