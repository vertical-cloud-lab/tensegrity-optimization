\subsection*{Budget Justification}

\textbf{Undergraduate stipends (\$15,000):}
The largest budget item supports 2--3 undergraduate research assistants across
the two-year project.  Stipends provide meaningful compensation for the
significant time commitment required for simulation development, fabrication,
and experimental campaigns.

\textbf{Graduate student mentoring (\$4,000):}
A small allocation supports a graduate student who serves as a day-to-day
co-mentor.  This student will provide hands-on guidance in simulation, coding,
fabrication, and experimental technique, amplifying the mentoring capacity of
the faculty PI.

\textbf{Fabrication materials (\$1,500):}
3D printing filament (PLA, PETG, or nylon) for rigid bar components, cables and
strings of calibrated stiffness, and assembly hardware (fasteners, jigs,
adhesives).  Heavier procurement occurs in Year~1 during initial fabrication
development.

\textbf{Sensors and data acquisition (\$1,500):}
One-time purchase of miniature load cells, a USB-based data acquisition module,
and optional accelerometers for impact characterization.  These are consumable-
scale items that remain with the lab for future student use.

\textbf{Software and computing (\$500):}
All primary software tools are open-source (Python, FEniCS, BoTorch).  A small
allocation covers cloud computing credits if simulation campaigns exceed local
workstation capacity.

\textbf{Conference travel (\$2,000):}
Supports one undergraduate student presenting research results at a national
conference (e.g., ASME IMECE) in Year~2.  Covers registration, airfare, and
lodging.

\textbf{Miscellaneous (\$500):}
Covers poster printing, lab consumables, and unforeseen expenses.
