The campaign surrogate with the review's fixes applied

PR #121, branch claude/request-c-tables-logocv-ai-20261027. Generated by analysis/bo-review/plot_fixes.py from committed run records; nothing is refitted on this page. Hover over any point or bar for its record. Every number here is in scorecard.json.

Which fixes reach the surrogate. Of the review's points, the noise model (item 1: noise learned, or replicate-based SEMs, instead of the per-article SEMs as fixed noise) and the filament settings (item 4: the four batch-level settings dropped) change the model. The objective thresholds and the filament point fixed before the acquisition (items 2 and 3) change only how candidates are chosen, so they do not enter the held-out predictions.

1. Held-out predictions: campaign surrogate against the same SAASBO with the noise learned

Leave-one-design-out cross-validation over the 44 articles: 35 design folds, each batch-3 reprint pair held out together, one NUTS refit per fold at the campaign budget (512 warmup steps, 256 samples). Both rows are the campaign's Ax 0.5.0 SAASBO with the same folds, seeds and budget; the bottom row blanks the SEMs so the noise is learned. Vertical bars are one posterior sd of the held-out prediction (latent, without the learned noise), horizontal bars the ingested SEM, dashed line parity. R²oos is against predicting each fold's training mean.

Reading. With the SEMs as fixed noise every held-out prediction sits near the pooled mean (spread of the predictions 0.32 of the data sd for t180, 0.11 for Ereb), and the Ereb ordering runs against the measurements (ρs −0.40). With the noise learned the predictions follow the measurements: t180 ρs +0.55 (p < 0.001), R²oos +0.40; Ereb ρs +0.51 (p < 0.001), R²oos +0.23. Its largest misses (measured, predicted): t180 r2d2c3 (1.33, 1.10); t180 drran7 (1.25, 1.03); Ereb (mJ) drran1 (18.09, 9.88); Ereb (mJ) corny9 (18.09, 10.65); Ereb (mJ) 2dran9 (15.13, 7.80).

3. The other fixes: replicate-based SEMs, and the filament settings dropped

Top: train_Yvar = SEM² + s_print², with s_print pooled over the reprint twins of each training fold (twelve inputs). Bottom: noise learned and the four filament settings dropped (eight inputs: R, H, θ, ds, dt, printed mass, strut and tendon infill), the model the review recommends until a plate varies the filament settings on purpose. Both: BoTorch 0.18.1 SAAS, NUTS 512/256.

4. Feature importance of the surrogate fitted to all 44 articles

SAAS fits to all 44 articles at the campaign budget, four NUTS seeds each (64 posterior draws pooled). Top: the share of model sensitivity, i.e. Ax's feature_importances (inverse length scale, normalized to sum to 1); bars are the posterior median over draws, whiskers the interquartile range; hover for Ax's pooled value and the value per seed. Bottom: the raw length scales on the unit cube, which say how smooth the model is (the normalization above hides that). Filled: noise learned. Open: SEMs as fixed noise (campaign).

Reading. With the SEMs as fixed noise the printed mass gets the largest share for both objectives, at length scales of a few hundredths of the cube: the model uses mass to tell the reprint twins apart and is flat between articles. With the noise learned every length scale is of the order of the cube or longer (a smooth, close to linear model), the t180 model ranks cable diameter dt first (Cable diameter dt in 4 of 4 seeds), and printed mass drops to an ordinary share. Ereb has no stable ranking: its top input changes with the seed.
