\section{Research Motivation and Overview}
% =============================================================================

We propose a two-year \textbf{experiment-driven} mentored research program in
which 2 undergraduate students design, fabricate, and test \textbf{multi-material
3D-printed tensegrity-inspired structures} for energy absorption.  Tensegrity
\DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{(tensional integrity) }\DIFdelend structures---assemblies of rigid compression members
suspended within a continuous network of tension members---exhibit remarkable
strength-to-weight ratios and tunable mechanical responses, making them
attractive for protective equipment, packaging, and aerospace applications
\DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{\mbox{%DIFAUXCMD
\citep{skelton2009tensegrity, fraternali2015tensegrity}}\hskip0pt%DIFAUXCMD
}\DIFdelend \DIFaddbegin \DIFadd{\mbox{%DIFAUXCMD
\citep{skelton2009tensegrity}}\hskip0pt%DIFAUXCMD
}\DIFaddend .
Recent work has shown that \emph{tensegrity-inspired} architectures can be
directly 3D-printed and retain desirable load-limiting behavior under impact
\citep{pajunen2019design}.  Because multimaterial FDM TPU elements are not
ideal cables, we target \DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{tensegrity-inspired }\DIFdelend architectures that reproduce key
tensegrity-like mechanical responses while remaining manufacturable; notably,
TPU's rate-dependent damping is an \emph{asset} for energy absorption rather
than merely a deviation from idealized behavior.

\DIFaddend Multi-material 3D printing with PLA (rigid struts) and TPU (flexible tension
elements) enables rapid fabrication of diverse tensegrity-inspired geometries
\DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{on
a single print platform }\DIFdelend \citep{ye2023multimaterial, khatri2024energy}.  Rather
than relying on costly \DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{simulation campaigns}\DIFdelend \DIFaddbegin \DIFadd{simulations}\DIFaddend , we leverage this manufacturing
agility \DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{to pursue }\DIFdelend \DIFaddbegin \DIFadd{for }\DIFaddend a \textbf{high-throughput experimental approach}: students
will print, test, and iterate on many candidate designs---we estimate 50--100+
unique specimens over two years---generating high-quality physical data
directly.  \textbf{Bayesian optimization} (BO) will guide the experimental
search, selecting the most informative designs to \DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{print and }\DIFdelend test next based on
prior results \citep{shahriari2016taking, frazier2018tutorial}.  This
experiment-first strategy produces reliable \DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{, real-world }\DIFdelend performance data while
keeping the project accessible to undergraduates \DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{at all levels---students }\DIFdelend \DIFaddbegin \DIFadd{where students }\DIFaddend can
contribute meaningfully through hands-on printing and testing without
\DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{requiring
}\DIFdelend advanced modeling expertise.

\textbf{A primary objective \DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{of this project }\DIFdelend is to provide a rich,
interdisciplinary mentored research experience for undergraduates.}  Through
weekly one-on-one mentoring, structured lab meetings, and \DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{a progressive
scaffolding model that moves students }\DIFdelend \DIFaddbegin \DIFadd{progressive
scaffolding }\DIFaddend from guided tasks to independent research
ownership, each student will gain \DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{hands-on }\DIFdelend skills in CAD, multi-material 3D
printing, mechanical testing, and data-driven optimization. Each student will \DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{be responsible for defining }\DIFdelend \DIFaddbegin \DIFadd{define }\DIFaddend design variables, \DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{executing experimental or computational campaigns, analyzing data, presenting }\DIFdelend \DIFaddbegin \DIFadd{execute experimental campaigns, analyze data, present }\DIFaddend results, and draft\DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{ing}\DIFdelend  manuscript sections.

\DIFdelend \textbf{Tensegrity structures} consist of isolated compression members (struts)
held in equilibrium by a continuous network of tension members
\citep{skelton2009tensegrity, sultan2009tensegrity}.  Their defining
characteristic---no two rigid bars touch---yields lightweight assemblies with
surprising load-bearing capacity and tunable nonlinear responses
\DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{\mbox{%DIFAUXCMD
\citep{amendola2014experimental, zhang2015tensegrity}}\hskip0pt%DIFAUXCMD
}\DIFdelend \DIFaddbegin \DIFadd{\mbox{%DIFAUXCMD
\citep{amendola2014experimental, zhang2018tensegrity}}\hskip0pt%DIFAUXCMD
}\DIFaddend .
Pajunen et al.\ \citep{pajunen2019design} demonstrated that
\emph{tensegrity-inspired} unit cells can be directly 3D-printed and exhibit
load-limiting plateaus under drop-weight impact.
Multi-material rigid--soft printing
\citep{ye2023multimaterial, khatri2024energy} has shown that PLA--TPU
combinations \DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{printed }\DIFdelend on a single FDM platform achieve tunable energy
absorption \DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{.  Ye et al.\ demonstrated }\DIFdelend \DIFaddbegin \DIFadd{via }\DIFaddend a wrapping-based strategy (rigid cores
encapsulated by continuous soft skins) that prevents interface delamination and
enables cyclic durability---a critical enabler for our fabrication approach.

\DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{These manufacturing advances mean that undergraduates can design, print, and
test many configurations in rapid succession, making 3D printing the natural
engine of experimental exploration.

}\DIFdelend 

\textbf{Bayesian optimization} (BO) is a sequential, model-based strategy for
optimizing expensive black-box functions using a Gaussian process surrogate
\citep{shahriari2016taking, frazier2018tutorial}.  BO has driven breakthroughs
across domains---from tuning hyperparameters of AlphaGo
\citep{silver2016mastering} to optimizing sustainable concrete formulations
\citep{ament2023sustainable} and discovering \DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{state-of-the-art }\DIFdelend organic laser
emitters \citep{striethkalthoff2024delocalized}---and has been applied to
metamaterial design \DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{\mbox{%DIFAUXCMD
\citep{wang2022bayesian}}\hskip0pt%DIFAUXCMD
}\DIFdelend \DIFaddbegin \DIFadd{\mbox{%DIFAUXCMD
\citep{mo2023accelerated}}\hskip0pt%DIFAUXCMD
}\DIFaddend .  In our framework, BO operates
\emph{directly on experimental data}: each round of printing and testing
\DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{provides observations that update }\DIFdelend \DIFaddbegin \DIFadd{updates }\DIFaddend a Gaussian process surrogate, which \DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{then
}\DIFdelend recommends the next \DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{set of }\DIFdelend designs to fabricate.  This closed-loop
print--test--optimize cycle eliminates the need for simulation \DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{as a prerequisite
}\DIFdelend and
keeps the workflow centered on physical \DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{experimentation---an approach that
is both scientifically rigorous and naturally }\DIFdelend \DIFaddbegin \DIFadd{experimentation---naturally }\DIFaddend suited to mentoring undergraduates\DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{at all experience levels}\DIFdelend .

\section{Student Research Project~1: Design, Fabrication, and Testing}
% =============================================================================

\textbf{Student profile:} One sophomore or junior undergraduate.  No prior
simulation or advanced modeling experience is required; the hands-on nature of
this project makes it an ideal entry point for students early in their studies.

\subsection*{Scope and Deliverables}

This student will lead the design-and-build cycle that forms the experimental
backbone of the project.  Working under weekly \DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{one-on-one }\DIFdelend mentoring from
Prof.~Hill, the student will \DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{begin by }\DIFdelend \textbf{\DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{parameterizing }\DIFdelend \DIFaddbegin \DIFadd{parameterize }\DIFaddend a family of
tensegrity-inspired unit cells} in CAD (SolidWorks or Fusion~360), defining key
variables---strut diameter and length (PLA), tension-element cross-section
(TPU), connectivity topology, and unit-cell tiling---that span the design space.
Following \citet{ye2023multimaterial}, the designs use a core-wrapping strategy
(rigid PLA struts encapsulated by continuous TPU skins) to ensure robust
mechanical interlocking\DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{and prevent interface delamination. Because PLA???TPU interfaces are known to exhibit mixed-mode fracture behavior and potential void formation, print parameter validation and }\DIFdelend \DIFaddbegin \DIFadd{. PLA--TPU }\DIFaddend interface robustness will be \DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{explicitly evaluated during early testing}\DIFdelend \DIFaddbegin \DIFadd{validated during early print trials}\DIFaddend .

The student will then \textbf{fabricate structures} using the department's
multi-material FDM printer, \DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{systematically }\DIFdelend iterating on print parameters
\DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{(layer height, infill, temperature) }\DIFdelend to achieve reliable geometries\DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{in both PLA
and TPU.
By printing many candidate designs quickly, the student builds an
expanding library of tested specimens and develops practical expertise in
additive manufacturing---a transferable skill valued in both graduate research
and industry.  }\DIFdelend \DIFaddbegin \DIFadd{.
}\DIFaddend The student works closely with Student~2, who tests each
batch and returns performance data\DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{; this tight collaboration teaches both
students the value of interdisciplinary teamwork and creates natural
peer-mentoring opportunities as each develops complementary expertise.
Throughout, }\DIFdelend \DIFaddbegin \DIFadd{.
}\DIFaddend Prof.~Baird's master's student serves as a day-to-day co-mentor,
guiding \DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{the student through equipment operation , troubleshooting print failures,
and interpreting early results, so that learning happens at multiple cadences:
weekly faculty meetings for strategy and daily peer support for execution}\DIFdelend \DIFaddbegin \DIFadd{equipment operation and troubleshooting}\DIFaddend .

\textbf{Mentoring outcomes:} The student will develop skills in CAD,
multi-material additive manufacturing, and systematic experimental design.
Structured mentoring scaffolds move the student from guided replication of
existing designs to independent creation of novel geometries by mid-Year~1.
The student will present results at \textbf{UCUR} or \textbf{ASME IDETC} and
contribute as co-author on a journal manuscript.

\section{Student Research Project~2: Mechanical Testing and Data-Driven Optimization}
% =============================================================================

\textbf{Student profile:} One sophomore, junior, or senior undergraduate with
coursework or interest in mechanics of materials or data analysis.

\subsection*{Scope and Deliverables}

This student will lead physical testing and the Bayesian optimization
campaign, working under weekly one-on-one mentoring from Prof.~Baird.  The work
begins with \textbf{\DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{conducting }\DIFdelend experimental testing} on structures fabricated by
Student~1: quasi-static compaction \DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{tests }\DIFdelend and drop-weight impact tests following
\DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{the protocol of }\DIFdelend \citet{pajunen2019design}.  Primary metrics include \emph{peak transmitted
force}, \emph{specific energy absorption} (SEA), and \emph{compaction
efficiency}\DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{---tangible,
operationally measurable outcomes well suited to undergraduate research}\DIFdelend .

With \DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{an initial data set }\DIFdelend \DIFaddbegin \DIFadd{initial data }\DIFaddend in hand, the student will \textbf{implement a \DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{single-fidelity Bayesian optimization }\DIFdelend \DIFaddbegin \DIFadd{BO }\DIFaddend loop}
(BoTorch/Ax) that operates directly
on experimental measurements.  After each \DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{BO }\DIFdelend iteration the surrogate recommends
the next batch of designs; the student coordinates with Student~1 to print and
test them, closing the loop.  \DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{This closed-loop
}\emph{\DIFdel{design--print--test--optimize}} %DIFAUXCMD
\DIFdel{cycle teaches the student data-driven
decision-making in a concrete, physical context.  If time and student interest
permit}\DIFdelend \DIFaddbegin \DIFadd{If time permits}\DIFaddend ,
lightweight simulations may be added as \DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{an optional }\DIFdelend \DIFaddbegin \DIFadd{a }\DIFaddend low-fidelity data
source to accelerate convergence \citep{mo2023accelerated}, but the campaign
does not depend on simulation\DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{fidelity}\DIFdelend .

Prof.~Baird's master's student provides day-to-day co-mentoring in Python,
data analysis, and BO implementation\DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{, creating a layered mentoring structure}\DIFdelend .
In Year~2, both students mentor newly recruited undergraduates, reinforcing
their own understanding while sustaining the research pipeline.

\textbf{Mentoring outcomes:} The student will develop skills in experimental
mechanics (test design, data acquisition), scientific computing (Python),
and data-driven optimization.  They will present results at \textbf{ASME IDETC}
or \textbf{UCUR} and contribute as co-author on a journal manuscript.

\section{Mentoring Environment}
\label{sec:mentoring}
% =============================================================================

Each student \DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{in this program }\DIFdelend follows a structured path from guided exploration
to independent research ownership, supported by layered mentoring\DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{at every
stage}\DIFdelend .
Because the project \DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{is centered }\DIFdelend \DIFaddbegin \DIFadd{centers }\DIFaddend on hands-on experimentation---designing,
printing, and testing physical structures---students engage with tangible
problems from day one\DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{, building confidence and competence through rapid,
concrete feedback loops}\DIFdelend .

\subsection*{Recruitment}

Students will be recruited from courses in mechanics of materials, machine
design, kinematics, and compliant mechanisms, targeting sophomores through
seniors.  The experiment-driven nature of the project lowers the barrier to
entry: students need not have prior simulation or programming experience\DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{,
broadening the pool of potential mentees}\DIFdelend .

\subsection*{Mentoring Structure}

Students meet individually with Profs.~Hill and Baird each week (30~min) for
technical guidance and professional development, and participate in weekly lab
group meetings (1~hr) that include progress updates, \DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{student }\DIFdelend literature
presentations, and skill-building workshops \DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{covering }\DIFdelend \DIFaddbegin \DIFadd{on }\DIFaddend scientific writing,
presentation skills, and research ethics.  Prof.~Baird's master's student
serves as a graduate co-mentor, providing day-to-day guidance in 3D printing,
testing\DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{procedures}\DIFdelend , coding, and data interpretation\DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{; this layered model ensures
timely support while the graduate student develops their own mentoring skills}\DIFdelend .
Students begin with structured tasks---replicating known tensegrity
geometries and running standard test protocols---and progress to independent
design decisions, culminating in ownership of their research project. Year~1 focuses on \DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{technical }\DIFdelend skill acquisition and guided execution; Year\DIFaddbegin \DIFadd{~}\DIFaddend 2 emphasizes independent hypothesis generation, experimental design, and peer mentoring \DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{. In
Year~2, experienced students mentor }\DIFdelend \DIFaddbegin \DIFadd{of }\DIFaddend newly recruited sophomores\DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{, reinforcing
their own knowledge and creating a sustainable pipeline of trained researchers}\DIFdelend .

\subsection*{Student Development Outcomes}

\DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{The experiment-driven workflow structures mentoring }\DIFdelend \DIFaddbegin \DIFadd{Mentoring is structured }\DIFaddend around concrete milestones:
a student's first successful multi-material print, first compression test,
first data set fed into the BO loop.  \DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{On the }\DIFdelend \emph{\DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{technical}\DIFdelend \DIFaddbegin \DIFadd{Technical}\DIFaddend } \DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{side, students
gain experience in experimental mechanics(test design, data acquisition),
}\DIFdelend \DIFaddbegin \DIFadd{skills include
experimental mechanics, }\DIFaddend additive manufacturing (CAD, multi-material 3D printing\DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{with wrapping
strategies}\DIFdelend ),
and data-driven optimization (Python, Bayesian optimization).
\emph{Communication} skills develop through lab notebooks, group presentations,
conference talks, and co-authoring a peer-reviewed manuscript.
\emph{Professional} development includes project management, data-driven
decision-making\DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{(interpreting optimization recommendations and selecting next
experiments)}\DIFdelend , interdisciplinary collaboration, and preparation for graduate
school or industry careers.

\section{Potential Impact of Work}

This project advances architected material design for energy absorption with
applications in \textbf{protective equipment} (helmets, body armor),
\textbf{packaging} (shock-absorbing inserts), and \textbf{aerospace}
(lightweight impact-resistant structures).  \DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{By generating a large,
}\DIFdelend \DIFaddbegin \DIFadd{The }\DIFaddend systematically collected
experimental data set \DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{of tensegrity-inspired
structures, we provide }\DIFdelend \DIFaddbegin \DIFadd{provides }\DIFaddend the community with a resource for benchmarking
and further optimization.  The experiment-driven \DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{Bayesian optimization }\DIFdelend \DIFaddbegin \DIFadd{BO }\DIFaddend framework
demonstrates that undergraduates can \DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{meaningfully }\DIFdelend drive a closed-loop
experimental campaign, establishing a replicable mentoring model for
data-driven research\DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{in other metamaterial classes.  The layered
mentoring structure---faculty, graduate co-mentor, and peer
mentoring---can be adopted by other research groups seeking to involve
undergraduates in hands-on engineering research.
}\DIFdelend \DIFaddbegin \DIFadd{.  }\DIFaddend Open-source dissemination of data, code, and tested designs extends
impact beyond BYU\DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{and enables other groups---including primarily
undergraduate institutions---to build on this work}\DIFdelend .

\textbf{Year~1} focuses on experimental foundations: \DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{student }\DIFdelend recruitment and
onboarding in Fall, \DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{followed by literature review , tensegrity parameterization
in CAD, and initial multi-material print trials through Winter.  The first
Summer term is a critical period }\DIFdelend \DIFaddbegin \DIFadd{literature review and CAD parameterization through Winter, and a Summer term }\DIFaddend of full-time research with intensive
\DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{mentoring---faculty increase meeting frequency to twice weekly, and the
graduate co-mentor works alongside students daily.  Students }\DIFdelend \DIFaddbegin \DIFadd{mentoring where students }\DIFaddend ramp up
fabrication\DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{throughput}\DIFdelend , run systematic compression and \DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{drop-weight }\DIFdelend impact
tests, and begin feeding results into the \DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{Bayesian optimization }\DIFdelend \DIFaddbegin \DIFadd{BO }\DIFaddend loop.
\textbf{Year~2} shifts to optimization and dissemination: BO-guided
\DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{experimental }\DIFdelend campaigns continue through Fall and Winter \DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{, with students printing
